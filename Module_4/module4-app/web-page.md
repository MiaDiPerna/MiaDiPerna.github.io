# Web page

## Headline
[ONE LINE.]
One live ticket count, from every website you sell on.

## Subhead
[ONE OR TWO SENTENCES.]
SeatSync is a phone app for sports operations managers. It adds up ticket sales from every website, so you always know how many seats are left.

## The problem, in the user's words
[HOW SOMEONE IN YOUR FIELD WOULD DESCRIBE IT TO A COWORKER.]
"We sell tickets on a few different websites, and none of them talk to each other. To get a real total, I'm copying numbers into a spreadsheet, and by the time I'm done it's already out of date. That's how we end up overselling a game, or pulling promotions for a game that wasn't really sold out. And at the end of the month, I'm still trying to figure out whether every site paid us what it owed."

## How the app helps
[THREE POINTS. ONE SENTENCE EACH.]
- **One live total:** see tickets sold and seats left for every game, across every website, on one screen.
- **No overselling:** when a seat sells on one website, SeatSync takes it off the others, and it warns you when only 10% of seats are left.
- **No missed payouts:** SeatSync checks each website's payout against its sales and flags any that don't match.

## What to do next
[THE ONE THING YOU WANT THE READER TO DO.]
Try SeatSync. One button: **Open SeatSync**, which takes the reader to the app.

[COLORS, TONE, ANY IMAGE YOU WANT ON IT.]
