# Module 4 App

This folder holds the Module 4 project: a problem, the evidence for it, a proposed solution, and the materials that pitch it.

## Files (work through them in order)

1. `problem.md`: the problem being solved and who has it
2. `evidence.md`: data, sources, and interviews showing the problem is real
3. `survey.md`: survey sent to team ticket/operations staff (answers due Thursday, Nov 12, 2026). When the answers arrive, use them with `solution.md` and change the plan wherever they disagree with it.
4. `solution.md`: the proposed app and how it addresses the problem
5. `web-page.md`: copy for the app's landing page
6. `ad-script.md`: script for a short ad promoting the app

## Guidelines for Claude

- Keep every file consistent with `problem.md` and `solution.md`. If those change, update the others.
- Cite every claim in `evidence.md` with a source.
- Don't invent statistics, quotes, or sources. Leave a `TODO` instead.
- Keep the app tiny. Build only what `solution.md` lists under "The screens," and never anything under "What it does not do." Don't add features, screens, or alerts the user didn't choose. Ask first.
- Before any big decision on something the user hasn't clearly stated, stop and ask. Don't decide and report afterward.
