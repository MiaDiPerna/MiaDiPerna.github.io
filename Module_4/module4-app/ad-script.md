# Ad script

## Length
[30, 45 OR 60 SECONDS.]
45 seconds.

## The scenes
| Seconds | On screen | Voice or text | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | **[THE HOOK]** An operations manager in a team polo stands in a stadium hallway, holding three phones and a laptop. Each screen shows a different ticket website. One phone slips and falls. | Manager, flustered: "How many tickets did we sell?" | All the phones buzzing at once. A cartoon "boing" as the phone drops. |
| 5–15 | **[THE PROBLEM MOMENT]** At a desk covered in sticky notes, the manager copies numbers into a giant spreadsheet. A coworker leans in the door. | Coworker: "Hey… we sold the same seats twice." The manager slowly puts their head on the desk. Text on screen: "Sound familiar?" | Fast keyboard clacking that stops suddenly. A sad trombone "wah-wah." |
| 15–35 | **[THE APP SOLVING IT]** The manager, now calm, holds one phone showing the SeatSync Home screen: each game with tickets sold and seats left. Three quick moments: (1) a seat sells on one website and disappears from the others, (2) a notification: "10% of seats left," (3) the Payouts screen with one website marked "Flagged." | Voiceover: "SeatSync puts every ticket website in one live count. It takes sold seats off the other sites, warns you when seats run low, and flags any payout that doesn't match." | Light, upbeat music starts. A soft "ding" for each of the three moments. |
| 35–45 | **[THE CLOSING LINE]** The manager leans back with one phone, smiling. The other phones sit in a drawer labeled "Retired." The SeatSync name appears in navy on white. | Voiceover: "SeatSync. One count. Every website." Text on screen: "SeatSync" | Music ends on a bright final note. |

## Tone
[FUNNY, SERIOUS, CALM, FAST. PICK ONE.]
Funny.

I picked the funny hook because ticket counts and spreadsheets are a dry topic, and humor makes a boring problem fun to watch.
