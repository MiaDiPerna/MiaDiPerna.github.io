About

I'm a senior at Xavier University double majoring in Business Management and Liberal Arts. I grew up in Medina, Ohio, and came to Xavier to compete as a Division I student-athlete while building a strong academic foundation in business.

Before Xavier, I completed coursework toward a Business Administration degree with a minor in International Business at Kent State University from 2023 to 2025, as an Honors College student and a member of the Women's Lacrosse team. That experience shaped the discipline and drive I brought with me when I transferred to Xavier.

At Xavier, I serve as team captain of the Women's Lacrosse team, competing in the Big East Conference. In summer 2025, I also studied abroad in Florence, Italy.

Balancing D1 athletics with a double major has taught me discipline, time management, and how to perform under pressure — skills I bring to academics, teamwork, and everything in between.

Student-Athlete

I've played lacrosse for as long as I can remember. From 2023 to 2025 I competed as a member of the Women's Lacrosse team at Kent State University, and that's the reason I came to Xavier University to continue competing at the Division I level in the Big East Conference. Today I serve as team captain, leading both on and off the field, and balance 20+ hours of Division I athletics commitments weekly with a full-time academic course load. Between practices, lifts, travel, and games, lacrosse has demanded a level of discipline and time management that's carried over into everything else I do.

This past season, I suffered a season-ending injury — I tore my ACL and was out for the entire season. Rehabbing while still supporting my team as captain from the sideline was one of the hardest challenges I've faced, and it's taught me resilience I couldn't have learned any other way.

More than the competition itself, I love what the sport has given me: a team to grow alongside, a reason to push past what I thought I was capable of, and lessons in resilience that no classroom could teach.
