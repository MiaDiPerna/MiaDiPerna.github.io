About

I'm a senior at Xavier University double majoring in Business Management and Liberal Arts. I grew up in Medina, Ohio, and came to Xavier to compete as a Division I student-athlete while building a strong academic foundation in business.

Before Xavier, I completed coursework at Kent State University from 2023 to 2025, majoring in General Business with a minor in International Business, as a member of the Women's Lacrosse team. That experience shaped the discipline and drive I brought with me when I transferred to Xavier.

At Xavier, I serve as team captain of the Women's Lacrosse team, competing in the Big East Conference. In summer 2025, I also studied abroad in Florence, Italy.

Balancing D1 athletics with a double major has taught me discipline, time management, and how to perform under pressure — skills I bring to academics, teamwork, and everything in between.
