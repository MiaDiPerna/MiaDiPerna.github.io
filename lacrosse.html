Lacrosse

Lacrosse has been one of the biggest parts of my life — it's what brought me to Xavier and shaped who I am as a student and a teammate.

My Story

I've played lacrosse for as long as I can remember, and it's the reason I came to Xavier University to compete at the Division I level. Between practices, lifts, travel, and games, lacrosse has demanded a level of discipline and time management that's carried over into everything else I do.

More than the competition itself, I love what the sport has given me: a team to grow alongside, a reason to push past what I thought I was capable of, and lessons in resilience that no classroom could teach.

What It's Taught Me

Discipline

Balancing a D1 schedule with a double major means every hour counts.

Teamwork

Learning to trust and lift up teammates, on and off the field.

Resilience

Competing at the highest level means learning to bounce back from setbacks.
